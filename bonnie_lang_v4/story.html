Music With Meaning

Two stories of survival. Two songs born from the deepest places. This is why the music exists.

"Teddy" — A Childhood Battle

When I was a child, I fought a battle with cancer that changed my perspective on life forever. "Teddy" is the story of that resilience—a song written for the little version of me who refused to give up, and for anyone currently in the fight of their lives.

Hear the Song See Photos

The Miracle (Dec 2024)

On December 14, 2024, my life stopped. I experienced a sudden cardiac arrest and was clinically gone for several minutes. Against all odds, I am here today to tell the story. This "miracle" has deepened my mission to live out loud and encourage others to chase their dreams while they still can.
