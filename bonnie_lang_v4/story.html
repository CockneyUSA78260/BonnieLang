My Story

Every song comes from somewhere. For me, they come from the road, from survival, and from a deep belief that it's never too late to start over. After overcoming significant health and personal hurdles, I realized that life isn't meant to be lived in one place. It's meant to be an adventure.

Selling my home and moving into an RV wasn't just about travel; it was about freedom. Freedom to play music where I want, meet people from all walks of life, and share the message that you should live while you can.
