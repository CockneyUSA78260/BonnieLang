Hi — I'm Bonnie Lang

Hi, I'm Bonnie Lang — a Texas-born singer-songwriter, storyteller, and full-time RV traveler chasing songs, sunsets, and stories across America.

I've had the wonderful opportunity of performing on stages across the country, appearing in national commercials, and on Paramount+ shows like Lawmen: Bass Reeves and 1923. I've also had the honor of opening for artists like Mark Chesnutt, The Bellamy Brothers, and several incredible Texas artists along the way. I'm a songwriter sharing life through music.

I released the deeply personal song "Teddy," inspired by my childhood battle with cancer at age nine. During that time, a small teddy bear became my symbol of hope—one I still carry with me today.

In December 2024, life added another unexpected chapter. While performing a Christmas show, I suffered a cardiac arrest and was clinically gone for 15 minutes. Thanks to quick-acting angels, and CPR, I'm still here. What followed was a long road of recovery—including ICD surgery, frozen shoulder, another surgery, and time away from the stage.

That experience gave birth to a new song, "Live While You Can," a powerful reminder to love deeply, dream boldly, and live fully—right now.

I travel full-time in an RV, sharing the adventure through my video podcast, Wheels and Warbles, while working toward my dream of performing in all 50 states.

I'm grateful for every mile, every stage, and every person who listens along the way. The fire never went out — and I can't wait to share the next song with you. I hope to meet you along the journey.

Peace, love, happiness, and big hugs,
Bonnie Lang
